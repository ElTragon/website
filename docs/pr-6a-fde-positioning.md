# PR 6A: FDE positioning and information architecture

This document records the content decisions behind the first stage of the
portfolio modernization. It is intentionally narrower than the later visual
redesign and case-study work.

## Positioning

Primary statement:

> Full-Stack Engineer for Complex Customer Problems

Supporting idea:

Mario works with customers and domain experts to turn complicated workflows
into software, including healthcare integrations, internal tools, and
full-stack products.

The site demonstrates capabilities associated with forward-deployed
engineering without claiming that Mario previously held that formal title.

## Audience and action

The primary audience is hiring managers and recruiters for forward-deployed,
solutions, and full-stack engineering roles. The primary homepage action is
**View Work**. Contact is the secondary action.

## Homepage information architecture

1. Positioning and primary actions
2. Measurable outcomes
3. Selected work
4. Working approach
5. About
6. Latest writing
7. Contact

The page leads with customer and operational outcomes rather than a list of
technologies.

## Public evidence

The initial homepage uses claims already present in the selected
field-solutions resume:

- HL7 tooling reduced client onboarding time by 50%.
- Research-platform page loads improved from five seconds to under one second.
- Twelve regulated applications supported more than 200 staff members.

Employer names, product names, technologies, resume metrics, generalized
workflows, and sanitized diagrams or screenshots are approved for future case
studies. Future content must remain supportable in an interview and must not
expose proprietary data, credentials, internal schemas, or confidential
implementation details.

## Routes

PR 6A links only to destinations that currently exist:

- `/` - positioning, selected work, About, and Contact
- `/blogs/` - writing archive
- `/resume.pdf` - field-solutions resume

Detailed `/work/` and `/work/[case-study]/` routes are deferred until the
case-study content model and source material are ready.

## Deferred work

- Full visual concept and design-system modernization
- Dedicated case-study routes and long-form content
- Sanitized architecture diagrams and screenshots
- Interactive integration demonstration
- Visual-regression and performance budgets for redesigned pages
